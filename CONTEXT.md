# Portfolio

Fitz's personal portfolio site, aimed at a tech career. Visual design lives in the Figma file "Portfolio - CMS".

## Language

**Owner**:
Fitz, a student at the Polytechnic University of the Philippines (expected graduation 2027) who builds web and mobile apps and small tools.

**Audience**:
Recruiters and hiring managers evaluating the Owner for tech roles.
_Avoid_: visitors, users

**Work**:
A piece of tech work the Owner shows, with a title, year, summary, stack tags, and optional live link.
_Avoid_: repo, app

**Cover**:
The image on a Work card. A photo when one exists, shown in full colour with a specular shine on its edge. Otherwise a generated colourful pattern, shown as monochrome dots until the Audience hovers and then revealed in colour around the cursor.

**Status badge**:
The plain pill on a Work Cover that has no photo, reading "Under development" with an icon. Shown on the Works page card of every Work without a photo, so it is never set by hand. The small Home thumbnail of that Work carries no badge. Distinct from the Award badge.
_Avoid_: tag, label

**Category**:
A label on a Work (Web, Mobile, Hackathon, Research) that the Works page filters by. A Work can have more than one.

**Award badge**:
The mark on a Work or Highlight that won something. Used only for wins and awards, never as a generic tag.

**Writing**:
An essay by the Owner, written as an MDX file in `content/writings/` (exporting `meta`: title, date, summary, tags, reading time), shown in the Writings index and on its own page. Writings are hidden from the site until a real one exists.

**Highlight**:
The single top achievement shown on Home: Champion and Most Innovative Prototype, PUP Hackathon 2026, with AGAPAI.

**Event**:
A hackathon or community occurrence the Owner took part in, shown in "Events & community".

**CREOMS**:
The Customer Relationship and E-commerce Ordering Management System the Owner leads development of for a client. The client is never named publicly.

**AGAPAI**:
Offline-first injury first-aid app with a health-office dashboard. Won Champion and Most Innovative Prototype at PUP Hackathon 2026 as Team AI Did It (team of five; the Owner was AI and lead developer).

**PACE**:
Pair-programming AI Coding Environment, a hint-only AI tutor research prototype from PUP students.

**INGAT Remit**:
Split-remittance app for OFWs on Stellar, built for the Stellar APAC Hackathon 2026.

## Relationships

- The portfolio shows tech work only; the Owner's art is out of scope.
- Writings are hidden until the Owner has a first essay, including their route, RSS feed, nav link, footer RSS link and the Home "Recent writings" section. They appear automatically once a Writing exists.
- Colour comes from photos; the interface itself stays neutral.
- Event photos and Work photos show in full colour at rest; Writing images stay muted until the Audience interacts. Work Covers without a photo are the exception: dots at rest, colour on interaction.
- Photos carry credit to their source (RISFI, Rise In and Stellar).
